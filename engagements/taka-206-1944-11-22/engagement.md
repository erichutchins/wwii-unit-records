The three boats' torpedo attacks of 22–23 November 1944, as typed on their attack sheets, beside the log the
convoy's minesweeper W-15 kept of the same night. Times are converted to GMT, so BANG and SHAD (zone K) and the
Japanese escorts (zone I) share one clock.

## Outcome

Three of the six ships were sunk (Hozan, Amakusa and Sakae Maru); Taiken, Sannin and Eikai Maru sheltered at Funauki
(W-15's signal log). BANG claimed four sunk and one damaged; REDFISH two sunk (her Attacks 1 and 3, 0104 and 0141
K); SHAD's one salvo, fired by mistake at a torpedo boat at 2338 K, missed. The postwar credit gives BANG Sakae and
Amakusa Maru and REDFISH Hozan Maru, but W-15 logged Hozan Maru torpedoed at 2339 I (0039 K), 25 minutes before
REDFISH first fired and seven minutes after BANG's Attack 1.

## The convoy

Keelung to Naha, sailed 22 November; ships and escorts from W-15's battle report. The convoy's own record:
[[convoy:taka-206]].

## Reading the map

Positions are each navigator's own dead reckoning; lines between them are straight. REDFISH's chronology position
for Hozan Maru (24-27N 122-47E) is her own Attack 3 position, not the target's.

## Clocks

BANG, REDFISH and SHAD all kept zone K (−10) that night (the pack agreed "all times ... will be minus ten (King)").
Japanese records use Tokyo time (I, −9): Manazuru's red flare at 2240 I is 2340 K, 52 minutes before BANG's Attack
1.

## Who sank Hozan Maru

REDFISH heard "first of many explosions; BANG apparently made her attack" at 0037 K and fired her own first
torpedoes at 0104 K. W-15 saw Hozan Maru torpedoed at 2339 I (0039 K). By the reports' own clocks the hit fits
BANG's attack, not REDFISH's; the US credit to REDFISH appears to rest on her Attack 1 or 3 claim.
