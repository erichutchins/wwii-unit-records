BANG's one torpedo attack of her fifth patrol, as typed on her attack sheet, with her depth log. Times are converted
to GMT, with Tokyo time alongside.

## Outcome

No Japanese record of this attack has been found yet.

## Reading the map

Positions are BANG's own dead reckoning and star sights; lines between them are straight, and where she went in
between is not recorded. Open circles are ships she sighted, at the target's position. As the clock passes each
firing time a red ring pulses at BANG's position; hover a diamond for the attack sheet.

## Clocks

BANG kept zone I (−9), Tokyo time, for this attack.

## What is missing

No Japanese record of this attack has been found yet. Of the pack, only ATULE was still in the Yellow Sea on 10
February: on the 8th BANG ordered her to cover both their areas until the 11th. SPADEFISH was further north (Port
Arthur, 6 Feb) and POMPON had left the area on 31 January after flooding her pump room. ATULE's report says nothing
of BANG's attack.
