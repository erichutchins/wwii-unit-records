BANG's first attacks: four approaches on convoy TAMA-17 west of Luzon over the night of 29–30 April, with PARCHE and
TINOSA closing in on her reports. Every mark is a position or attack typed into the boats' war patrol reports,
beside the Japanese record of the same minutes, on one GMT clock.

## Outcome

Japanese loss list: Takegawa Maru (1,930 GRT) and Nittatsu Maru (2,859 GRT), both to BANG. No loss matches PARCHE's
or TINOSA's attacks next morning; the rest reached Manila on 2 May.

## The convoy

Takao to Manila, sailed 1300 on 28 April. Its ships, as the escort division's table, Nittatsu Maru's movement log
and combinedfleet.com name them, are in the convoy's own record: [[convoy:tama-17]].

## Reading the map

Positions are the boats' own dead reckoning and star sights, and three navigators never quite agree, so the same
convoy appears in slightly different places in each report. Lines between positions are straight; where a boat
actually went in between is not recorded. Dashed outlines mark entries the report itself contradicts, kept with the
reason.

## Clocks

BANG kept zone K (−10) and PARCHE zone I (−9); TINOSA kept zone H (−8) on station. Japanese records use Tokyo time
(I, −9). On 29–30 April the target ship's own log and BANG's attack sheets agree to within one to seven minutes once
both are in GMT.
