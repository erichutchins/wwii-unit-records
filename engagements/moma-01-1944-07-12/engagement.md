THRESHER found a southbound convoy on the night of 11 July and the four boats closed on it. APOGON attacked first,
at 0304 on the 12th (Tokyo time), heard three hits and was rammed by a freighter of the convoy, bending her periscope
shears; PIRANHA attacked at 0656 and sank a troop transport. GUARDFISH and THRESHER tracked it and were driven off by
its escort. The boats' war patrol reports beside the escort division's record, on one GMT clock.

## Outcome

One ship sunk, Nichiran Maru, an Army transport of 6,503 tons: the escort division's chart marks her at 0745 on the
12th at N18-?9 E122-?0, the Navy's summary at 0935 (or 0735). No damage is recorded for APOGON's hour or for the ship
that rammed her. PIRANHA's attack is paired as a hit, APOGON's as no loss.

## The convoy

From Takao on the 9th for Manila, by the chart; its size and escorts are blank there. The name MOMA-01 heads the
chart's line; reading it as Moji–Manila is ours. Its own record: [[convoy:moma-01]].

## Reading the map

Positions are each boat's own reckoning. THRESHER is drawn because she found the convoy and tracked it; she did not
fire at it. GUARDFISH, the group commander's boat, tracked it too and is left off the map (the page draws three
boats); her track is on her own page.

## Clocks

APOGON kept zone I (−9), the same as the Japanese records; PIRANHA zone G (−7) and THRESHER zone H
(−8).
