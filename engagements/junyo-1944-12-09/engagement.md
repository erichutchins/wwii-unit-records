Three boats on one carrier's force in the night of 8–9 December 1944: SEA DEVIL's four stern torpedoes at 0028 I,
PLAICE's salvoes at the screen at 0128 and 0131 I, and REDFISH's attacks at 0156 and 0320 I, as typed on their attack
sheets, beside the Imperial Navy's summary, which has Junyō torpedoed once, two hits, at 0130, and the destroyer Maki hit in
the bow.

## Outcome

Junyō, returning from Mako, was torpedoed about 50 miles south-west of Nomozaki: two hits, but she could make 13 knots
on one shaft and entered Sasebo about 1000 (p. 5) [[cite:jacar:C16120660300]]. The same page has the destroyer Maki
hit once in the bow, 30 miles from Meshima, and back at Nagasaki at 1000, entered at 0200 in a block whose day is not
marked. The summary does not say whose torpedoes. Three boats' records can be set against it:

- SEA DEVIL timed two explosions at 0031:40 and 0031:55 I, each checking with a torpedo's run; PLAICE heard three at
  0030 by her own clock, "established ... as hits from the SEA DEVIL's attack". Two hits, as the summary has, an hour
  before its time.
- PLAICE fired three bow torpedoes at the leading destroyer at 0128 I, saw "a large carrier with island
  superstructure" come out of a rain squall at 0129, heard two hits in the destroyer at 0131, fired four stern
  torpedoes diving at the third destroyer, and heard two more hits at 0133. Her hits fit the summary's time; her
  targets, as she saw them, were the screen.
- REDFISH claimed one hit at 0156 I and one more at 0320 I nearer Nomozaki.

Every pairing is possible: which boat hit Junyō, and which Maki, is open until a record with their own times is found.

## Reading the map

Positions are each boat's own; lines between them are straight. Junyō is drawn from the boats' sightings, and no
Japanese position is given in figures.

## Clocks

SEA DEVIL's and PLAICE's sheets type zone I; REDFISH's Attack 7 sheet types a J that is read here as I (the reason is given with her attack, under Questions and sources). Japanese records use Tokyo time (I, −9).

## What is missing

Junyō's own record and her escorts' December diaries: none found on JACAR by her name or the 21st Destroyer Division's.
SEA POACHER and PIRANHA were in the area that night (SEA DEVIL's ship contacts) and are not in the kit.
