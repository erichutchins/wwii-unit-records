BANG's last two attacks of her third patrol, on a convoy she found by its escorts' sound pinging, as typed on her
attack sheets. The convoy was most likely TAKA-909, sailing again after BANG's attack of the 19th; its escort
W-15's diary has it at sea that night and enters no attack.

## Outcome

BANG claimed an AO and an AK sunk and one damaged, as FRUPAC relayed it. No Japanese record has a loss that night.
TAKA-909's escort W-15 sailed again from Keelung with the convoy at 06?0 I on the 20th (noon 25-01N 122-21E) and
anchored at Miyako at 1530 I on the 21st (noon 24-51.5N 124-45E), entering nothing for the night between: no attack,
no ship lost (p. 49–50) [[cite:jacar:C08030607800]]. By straight interpolation between those noon positions the
convoy was some 25–30 nm west-south-west of BANG when she fired (ours, inferred); BANG's own track that day is dead
reckoning too.

## The target

BANG made contact by the escorts' JK sound pinging at 35,000 yards.

## Reading the map

Positions are BANG's own dead reckoning and star sights; lines between them are straight, and where she went in
between is not recorded. Open circles are ships she sighted, at the target's position. As the clock passes each
firing time a red ring pulses at BANG's position; hover a diamond for the attack sheet.

## Clocks

BANG kept zone K (−10) throughout. Japanese records use Tokyo time (I, −9).

## Records with no time

Two Japanese records for this patrol give no time: combinedfleet.com's account of convoy No. 3905 and the 4th
Surface Escort Division's daily summary for TAKA-909 (W-15's diary gives TAKA-909's, 0820 I). Each is drawn at the time of the attack it matches, and marked
so. FRUPAC's intercept has No. 3905's ships "torp & sunk 1520/09" (1520 I = 1620 K, between Attacks 1 and 2).

## What is missing

No Japanese record names the convoy of Attacks 5 and 6 (20–21 September) or records them: the 4th Escort diary has
no attack on those days, and W-15's diary, for the convoy most likely attacked, none that night. CD-30's diary
(the convoy's other warship) has not been found on JACAR. Where the Japanese record names only the ships that were hit, the rest of the convoy is described as
BANG saw it.
