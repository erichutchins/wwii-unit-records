TINOSA found convoy TE-04 north of Luzon on the morning of 3 May and reported it to PARCHE and BANG; by the early
hours of the 4th the three boats had attacked it in turn, and five of its six ships were gone. The boats' war patrol
reports beside the escort division's record, on one GMT clock.

## Outcome

Japanese loss list: five ships, 30,542 GRT. Which boat sank which is still open; see the attack order in the log.

## The convoy

Yulin to Moji by way of Keelung, sailed 30 April with six ships; iron ore for Yawata (combinedfleet.com, secondary).
Its ships are in the convoy's own record: [[convoy:te-04]].

## Reading the map

Positions are the boats' own dead reckoning and star sights, and three navigators never quite agree, so the same
convoy appears in slightly different places in each report. Lines between positions are straight; where a boat
actually went in between is not recorded. Dashed outlines mark entries the report itself contradicts, kept with the
reason.

## Clocks

BANG kept zone K (−10) and PARCHE zone I (−9); TINOSA kept zone H (−8) on station. Japanese records use Tokyo time
(I, −9).
