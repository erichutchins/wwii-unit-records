BANG's first attack of her second patrol, on a tanker alone, as typed on her attack sheet. No Japanese record of it
has been found yet.

## Outcome

BANG saw one hit forward of the stack, the tanker list and settle by the stern, and her crew put out boats; she
claimed one 7,000–8,000-ton AO damaged. No Japanese record of the attack has been found. The Chichijima base
force's June signal log has nothing for the 14th or 15th about a tanker torpedoed or her crew landed, only a Navy
GHQ broadcast of a submarine "certainly present" at 1200 I at 22-30N 144-30E, four hours before BANG fired and some
80 nm east of her (p. 18) [[cite:jacar:C08030275500]]. The Navy's June summary has no entry for her either.

## Reading the map

Positions are BANG's own dead reckoning and star sights; lines between them are straight, and where she went in
between is not recorded. Open circles are ships she sighted, at the target's position. As the clock passes each
firing time a red ring pulses at BANG's position; hover a diamond for the attack sheet.

## Clocks

BANG kept zone K (−10) for Attack 1 and zone I (−9) for Attacks 2 and 3, as her report does; GROWLER kept I on
station, but SEAHORSE kept H (−8). Japanese records use Tokyo time (I, −9): the escort division's chart has HI-67's
two oilers hit at 1515, seven minutes after BANG's sheet.

## What is missing

No Japanese record has been found yet for Attacks 1 and 3; those actions show only the American side. Where the
Japanese record names only the ships that were hit, the rest of the convoy is described as the boats saw it.
