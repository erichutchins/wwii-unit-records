BANG's attacks on convoy No. 3905, bound from Yokosuka for Haha-jima, as typed on her attack sheets, with her depth
log and the Yokosuka Guard Squadron's diary of the same afternoon.

## Outcome

Tokiwasan Maru (1,804 tons) and Shoryu Maru (1,916 tons) sunk. The Yokosuka Guard Squadron's diary records both
"torpedoed and sunk" at 1520 Tokyo time on the 9th, four minutes after BANG's first salvo (jacar:C08030363600 p3),
but gives the place as 265° 10 nm from Sumisu-jima, about 188 nm north-east of BANG's attack; BANG's position agrees
with the postwar one, 28-58N 137-45E (combinedfleet.com). BANG claimed a medium AK and a medium AO.

## The convoy

Yokosuka to Haha-jima (secondary). BANG saw 1 AO, 2 AK and 2 escorts. The convoy's own record: [[convoy:no-3905]].

## Reading the map

Positions are BANG's own dead reckoning and star sights; lines between them are straight, and where she went in
between is not recorded. Open circles are ships she sighted, at the target's position. As the clock passes each
firing time a red ring pulses at BANG's position; hover a diamond for the attack sheet.

## Clocks

BANG kept zone K (−10) throughout. Japanese records use Tokyo time (I, −9).

## Records with no time

Neither Japanese record for this patrol gives a time: combinedfleet.com's account of convoy No. 3905 and the 4th
Surface Escort Division's daily summary for TAKA-909. Each is drawn at the time of the attack it matches, and marked
so. FRUPAC's intercept has No. 3905's ships "torp & sunk 1520/09" (1520 I = 1620 K, between Attacks 1 and 2).

## What is missing

No Japanese record has been found yet for Attacks 5 and 6 (20–21 September); the 4th Escort diary has no attack on
those days. Where the Japanese record names only the ships that were hit, the rest of the convoy is described as
BANG saw it.
