TILEFISH, alone in the Nansei Shoto, found convoy NAKA-303 by its escorts' echo-ranging off the Tokara Islands on the
morning of 5 March 1945 and fired three torpedoes at its second freighter. One hit the leading escort, Minesweeper
No. 15, which had turned toward the attack: her own battle report and TILEFISH's war patrol report, on one GMT clock.

## Outcome

W-15's report: at 1044 (Tokyo time) one torpedo hit her aft; she broke between frames 96 and 97, her engines and
rudder were wrecked and her depth charges caught fire (jacar:C08030608100 p9). TILEFISH's: "Heard explosion four
minutes after firing which did not check with torpedo run to AK. Observed CHIDORI escort on fire aft and down by
stern. He evidently zigged into our first torpedo" (rg38-74856069/p34). She took the escort for Hatsukari and
claimed her damaged. The escorts towed W-15 and beached her off Suwanosejima, where TILEFISH fired two more torpedoes
at her at 1707 (K); the escorts' depth charges followed both attacks. JANAC lists Minesweeper No. 15 sunk by a
United States submarine on 5 March at 29-30N 129-33E (janac:3/p23), TILEFISH's typed attack position to the minute.

## Reading the map

TILEFISH's attack sheet types her noon position for the attack, 29-30.0N 129-33.7E (p33; the narrative's 1200, p15).
W-15's position at 1044 is computed from the bearings on her report's sketch (p30): 29-34.8N 129-33.9E, 4.8 nm from
TILEFISH's typed position; TILEFISH put her at 3,900 yards. The narrative's "000° 5 miles from Akusekijima" is
another 3.3 nm off.

## Clocks

TILEFISH kept zone K (−10); W-15's report is in Tokyo time (I, −9). TILEFISH fired at 1139 K, 1039 I, and heard the
explosion at 1143 K, 1043 I; W-15 has the attack on her at 1043 I and the hit at 1044 I.
