HAMMERHEAD found the convoy before dawn on 30 July and attacked it; STEELHEAD tracked it south all day and reported
it to PARCHE. From 0332 on the 31st (Tokyo time) the two boats attacked it in turn off the north-west corner of
Luzon; PARCHE made eight attacks in thirty-four minutes. The boats' war patrol reports beside the Navy's summary of
the night, on one GMT clock.

## Outcome

The Navy's July summary: four ships sunk (Yoshino, Manko and Fuso Maru, and a fourth whose name is not read) and two
torpedoed and damaged (No. 1 Ogura Maru and Dakar Maru); the convoy withdrew for a time to Pangui Bay. No Japanese
record found gives a time or place for any one ship, so which attack sank which is open: each claim is paired as
possible in the boats' files. JANAC's postwar list puts three ships at PARCHE's position and two at STEELHEAD's,
which are the boats' own; it has Dakar Maru sunk.

## The convoy

Takao to Manila, sailed at 0500 on 29 July with sixteen ships and six escorts, carrying Army units and other
transport. It had come south to Takao from 13 to 21 July. Its own record: [[convoy:mi-11]].

## The pack

PARCHE, STEELHEAD and HAMMERHEAD formed Coordinated Attack Group 17.15 under Cdr L. S. Parks, who directed the boats
from PARCHE (STEELHEAD's report, p11 and p50). HAMMERHEAD's report is not in the kit yet. GUARDFISH, sometimes put in
this action, was in another pack (with PIRANHA and THRESHER under Capt. O'Regan); her report has her leaving the strait
on 21 July and reaching Midway on the 31st.

## Reading the map

Positions are the boats' own dead reckoning. Each typed one position for all its attack sheets: PARCHE 19-10N
120-58E, STEELHEAD 18-57N 120-50E, about 15 nm apart for attacks within the same hour, so at least one navigator was
well off. PARCHE's narrative types no positions: her track is from her aircraft and ship contact tables. Lines
between positions are straight.

## Clocks

Both boats kept zone I (−9) in the area, the same as the Japanese records (Tokyo time). PARCHE's last attack sheet
types "0420-1433"; her narrative's 0429 and 0433 are used, with the typed times kept.
