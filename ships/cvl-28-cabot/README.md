# USS Cabot (CVL-28): 6 April 1945, from her anti-aircraft action forms

No war diary covers 6 April: Cabot's April diary ([NARA 633431403](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/633431403)) starts on 12 April. Her report on the air operations of 18 March - 8 April 1945 ([NARA 139967685](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/139967685), 79 pages, dated 12 April 1945) carries a COMINCH F-01 AA-1 form for each attack, each typed with the ship's longitude and latitude and "Zone Time ..., Zone Minus Nine". Two are for 6 April, and `positions.csv` has both (`p38`, `p40`, read on the scan):

- 1227, 26-38N 129-27E: a Judy attacked the ship (p38);
- 1305, 26-50N 129-29E: a Zeke attacked the ship, strafed it, near miss (p40).

The 7 April forms (`p30` at 1420, `p42` at 1213) are not entered. These are two fixes at the times of attacks, not a day's track; the track starts at 1227. No 0800, 1200 or 2000 position for Cabot is typed in this report.

Task group: the report's Part II (`p04`) puts Cabot in Task Unit 58.3.1 (Essex, Bunker Hill, Cabot; Rear Admiral Sherman in Essex, Commander Task Group 58.3). On 9 April she was detached to form Task Unit 58.3.10 with Hancock, Haynsworth and Stembel for Ulithi. The report's summary says that on 6 April two different planes selected Cabot for suicide crashing and both were shot down with very slight damage (`p03`).

Other days are not transcribed (lead L49).

`source` is `action`: a position typed on an anti-aircraft action form, at the time of the attack, not a diary table.
