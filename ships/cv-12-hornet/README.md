# USS Hornet (CV-12): April 1945 war diary, 6 April only (so far)

`positions.csv` has the same columns as the other ships. Transcribed from the April 1945 war diary
([NARA 140006155](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/140006155), 13 pages): the 0800, 1200 and 2000
positions of 6 April 1945 (`p04`, read on the scan). The diary uses East longitude dates and -9 (zone I) times
throughout (`p01`). Hornet was the guide of Task Group 58.1 (Rear Admiral J. J. Clark aboard), with Bennington,
Belleau Wood and San Jacinto (`p01`, 1 April). Other days of April are not transcribed (lead L49).
