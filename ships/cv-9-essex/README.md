# USS Essex (CV-9): April 1945 war diary, 6 April only (so far)

`positions.csv` is transcribed from the April 1945 war diary ([NARA 140006935](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/140006935), 10 pages): the 0800, 1200 and 2000 positions of 6 April 1945 (`p03`, read on the scan). Zone stated: Typed "6 April 1945. Zone -(9).". Task Group 58.3. Other days are not transcribed (lead L49).
