# USS Randolph (CV-15): April 1945 war diary, 5-6 April (so far)

`positions.csv` has the same columns as the other ships. Transcribed from the April 1945 war diary
([NARA 140007383](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/140007383)): the noon and 2000 positions of 5 April (0800 "anchored", at
Ulithi) and the 0800, 1200 and 2000 positions of 6 April 1945 (`p04`, read on the scan). Underway from Ulithi 0700 on 5 April
"to sortie on Second War Cruise"; clocks set back to -9 (zone I) at 1800 on 5 April; 6 April "(Zone -9)": "O. T. C. and guide
in RANDOLPH, flagship of Task Group 58.2"; "0615 - U. S. S. ENTERPRISE, HICKOX, HUNT, MARSHALL, and MILLER joined formation.
RANDOLPH assumed guide"; base course 340°T, 23 knots. On 6 April the group was 330-500 nautical miles south-east of Okinawa,
not yet with the rest of Task Force 58 (lead L49).
