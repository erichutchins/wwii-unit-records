# USS Hancock (CV-19): April 1945 war diary, 6 April only (so far)

`positions.csv` is transcribed from the April 1945 war diary ([NARA 633427363](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/633427363), 13 pages):
the 0800, 1200 and 2000 positions of 6 April 1945 (`p06`, zone I stated), and the "approximate position" of the 0540 launch (`p05`).
The diary types each day's position block at the end of that day's entry: the block at the top of `p05` is 5 April's, and the one
on `p06` before the "7 April 1945" heading is 6 April's. Task Group 58.3 off Okinawa. Other days are not transcribed (lead L49).
