# USS Enterprise (CV-6): April 1945 war diary, 5-7 April (so far)

`positions.csv` has the same columns as the other ships. Transcribed from the April 1945 war diary
([NARA 633437380](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/633437380), `p02-p03`, read on the scan): sortied from Ulithi 0930 on 5 April
as "a detached unit of Task Group 58.2"; clocks to -9 at 1800; on 6 April "At 0645 took station in Task Group 58.2 in cruising
disposition 5-ROGER ... Guide is RANDOLPH, O.T.C. is Rear Admiral G.F. BOGAN, USN, in RANDOLPH" (Randolph's diary says 0615);
7 April fuelled from Enoree and was joined by Independence, Oakland, Flint and DesDiv 105. The 2000 position of 5 April is
taken as zone -9 (the clocks were set back at 1800; the day's heading says -10): see the row's note.
