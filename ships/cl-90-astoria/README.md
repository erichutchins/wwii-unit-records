# USS Astoria (CL-90): April 1945 war diary, 6 April only (so far)

`positions.csv` is transcribed from the April 1945 war diary ([NARA 140001860](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/140001860), 65 pages): the 0800, 1200 and 2000 positions of 6 April 1945 (`p12`, read on the scan). Zone assumed: pages 10 and 12 type none. Positions typed in degrees, minutes and seconds, recorded as typed. Task Group 58.3. Other days are not transcribed (lead L49).
