# USS Springfield (CL-66): April 1945 war diary, 6 April only (so far)

`positions.csv` is transcribed from the April 1945 war diary ([NARA 140000852](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/140000852), 23 pages): the 0800, 1200 and 2000 positions of 6 April 1945 (`p04`, read on the scan). Zone assumed: pages 02-04 type none. Task Group 58.3. Other days are not transcribed (lead L49).
