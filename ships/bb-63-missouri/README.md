# USS Missouri (BB-63): April 1945 war diary, 6 April only (so far)

`positions.csv` is transcribed from the April 1945 war diary ([NARA 140000011](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/140000011), 17 pages): the 0800, 1200 and 2000 positions of 6 April 1945 (`p05`, read on the scan). Zone assumed: The month's first entry (p03) types "1 April 1945 (Zone Time -9)"; no zone is repeated on 6 April. Task Group 58.4. Other days are not transcribed (lead L49).
