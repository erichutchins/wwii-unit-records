# USS North Carolina (BB-55): April 1945 war diary, 6 April only (so far)

`positions.csv` is transcribed from the April 1945 war diary ([NARA 139988591](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/139988591), 33 pages): the 0800, 1200 and 2000 positions of 6 April 1945 (`p06`, read on the scan). Zone assumed: No zone is typed on the 6 April entry (p05-p06). Task Group 58.3. Other days are not transcribed (lead L49).
