# USS Bunker Hill (CV-17): April 1945 war diary, 6 April only (so far)

`positions.csv` is transcribed from the April 1945 war diary ([NARA 633434642](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/633434642), 32 pages):
the 0800, 1200 and 2000 positions of 6 April 1945 (`p07`, read on the scan; zone (-9) stated in the date line). Task Group 58.3
off Okinawa; fuelled and re-armed the day before (5 April, p06-p07 narrative); the 6 April narrative is on p07-p08. Other days
are not transcribed (lead L49).
