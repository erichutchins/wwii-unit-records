# USS San Jacinto (CVL-30): April 1945 war diary, 6 April only (so far)

`positions.csv` is transcribed from the April 1945 war diary ([NARA 139992560](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/139992560), 20 pages): the 0800, 1200 and 2000 positions of 6 April 1945 (`p04`, read on the scan). Zone: the 6 April heading (p03) types "6 April (Zone -9)": stated. Other days are not transcribed (lead L49).

Task group on 6 April: the 6 April entry (`p03`) types "Task Group 58.2, in company with Task Force 58, in disposition 5-R, axis 030°T, HORNET guide". The 5 April entry (p03) and the 7 April entry (p04) both type "Task Group 58.1", also with Hornet as guide, and Hornet's own diary puts the four carriers of 58.1 together (NARA 140006155 p01; L49 log). The 5-R axis, the guide and the formation are the same on all three days, so 58.2 on the 6th is read as a typing slip for 58.1: an inference from the diary's own neighbouring entries. Her 6 April entry (`p04`) has planes diving on Bennington and Belleau Wood "in the Task Group", and Belleau Wood's own report is forwarded via CTG 58.1. Not resolved from Hornet's, Bennington's or Belleau Wood's 6 April pages, which were not re-read for this.
