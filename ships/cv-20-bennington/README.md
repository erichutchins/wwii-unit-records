# USS Bennington (CV-20): April 1945 war diary, 6 April only (so far)

`positions.csv` is transcribed from the April 1945 war diary ([NARA 633421783](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/633421783), 29 pages):
the 0800, 1200 and 2000 positions of 6 April 1945 (`p09`, read on the scan; zone (I) stated). The block closes the 6 April entry,
before the "7 April 1945" heading. Task Group 58.1 with Hornet as guide. Other days are not transcribed (lead L49).
