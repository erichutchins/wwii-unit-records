# USS Washington (BB-56): April 1945 war diary, 6 April only (so far)

`positions.csv` is transcribed from the April 1945 war diary ([NARA 139999393](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/139999393), 12 pages): the 0800, 1200 and 2000 positions of 6 April 1945 (`p04`, read on the scan). Zone stated: Typed "Friday, 6 April 1945 ( Zone -9 time )". Task Group 58.3. Other days are not transcribed (lead L49).
