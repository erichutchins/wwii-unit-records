# USS Intrepid (CV-11): April 1945 war diary, 6 April only (so far)

`positions.csv` is transcribed from the April 1945 war diary ([NARA 140058107](https://catalog.archives.gov/id/140058107), 9 pages):
the 0800, 1200 and 2000 positions of 6 April 1945 (`p03`, read on the scan). The diary has Task Group 58.4 en route to the
fueling rendezvous, joining Task Group 50.8 at 0720 and rearming from the Lassen 1033-1618. The zone is assumed (-9, I). Other days
are not transcribed (lead L49).
