## What happened

On the night of 22 March, "the Third Army had crossed ... without even so much as an artillery preparation."
[[cite:last/13/p270]] [[cite:last/13/p273]]

"Jodl signed the surrender at 0241, 7 May, to become effective at 2301, 8 May." [[cite:last/19/p475]]

## What it meant

"It was, for all the crumbling nature of the opposition, a logistical tour de force by the most highly motorized and
mechanized armies the world had ever known." [[cite:last/20/p480]]

"The only basic matter to be decided by the last offensive was not whether the Germans would be reduced to total
defeat, but when." [[cite:last/20/p480]] [[cite:last/20/p481]]

"As the last offensive came to an end, few if any who fought in it could have entertained any doubts as to the right
of their cause--they had seen at Buchenwald, Belsen, Dachau, and at a dozen other places, including little Ohrdruf,
what awful tyranny man can practice on his fellow man." [[cite:last/20/p481]]
