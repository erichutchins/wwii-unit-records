## What it was for

On 2 July the Combined Chiefs directed a three-division landing on 15 August. "The missions of the ANVIL forces were
to seize the ports of Toulon and Marseille and exploit northward to Lyon to support future Allied operations in
Western Europe." [[cite:riviera/1/p21]]

## What happened

The 45th Division was to land "over a number of small beaches along the northern and southern shores of the St.
Tropez gulf." "Although some of the 45th Division's Delta beaches did not have good exits to the interior, they
proved generally adequate on D-day." On the Army map from 15 August:
[[unit:45id-hq@1944-08-15|the 45th Division's command post]] and
[[unit:106aaa-d@1944-08-15|Battery D, 106th AAA AW Bn]]. [[cite:riviera/5/p78]] [[cite:riviera/11/p201]]

On the evening of 10 September "the first meeting occurred between reconnaissance troops of the OVERLORD and
Anvil-DRAGOON forces." [[cite:breakout/32/p702]]

## What it meant

"After 14 September, however, the plans of Patch and his principal commanders would have to conform to a larger
operational framework dictated by SHAEF. For all purposes the campaign of southern France was officially over and a
new one begun, one in which the Mediterranean-based Allied army would obviously play a lesser role than before."
[[cite:riviera/10/p193]] [[cite:riviera/10/p194]]
