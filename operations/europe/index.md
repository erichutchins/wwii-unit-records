## What it was for

The cross-Channel force was to be built up in the United Kingdom "with the object of mounting an operation with
target date 1 May 1944 to secure a lodgment on the Continent from which further offensive operations can be carried
out." [[cite:harrison/2/p69]]

## What it meant

"With the lights going on again all over Europe, the United States and its allies on 8 May celebrated V-E (Victory
in Europe) Day." [[cite:last/20/p476]]
