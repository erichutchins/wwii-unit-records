## What happened

"On the morning of 16 December the American forces in the path of the German counteroffensive comprised four and
two-thirds divisions with an effective strength of about 83,000 men." [[cite:cole/25/p650]]

"This was the state of the weather when, on 3 January, the Allies began their final counterattack." By the end of
January "the bulge created by the futile counteroffensive that had cost Germans and Americans alike so heavily was
erased." [[cite:cole/25/p650]] [[cite:last/2/p53]]

In Alsace, "between 5 and 25 January, the German Army undertook four additional multidivision offensives against
the U.S. Seventh Army and another against the First French Army". After the last attacks of 24-25 January, the
Seventh Army counterattacked, "with the 100th and 45th Divisions on the west"
([[unit:45id-hq@1945-01-25|the 45th Division's command post]]), and "on 26 January, with their reserves exhausted,"
the German command "finally called a halt to what had clearly become a battle of attrition."
[[cite:riviera/28/p513]] [[cite:riviera/28/p526]]

## What it meant

"Once the Germans had failed in the Ardennes, there could be no longer any doubt that they had lost the war."
