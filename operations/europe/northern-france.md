## What happened

"Again on the morning of 25 July the planes came. Flying in groups of twelve, over 1,500 B-17's and B-24's dropped
more than 3,300 tons of bombs in the COBRA area". [[cite:breakout/12/p234]]

"Thinly spread, both laterally and in depth, the armies overran and liberated northern France, most of Belgium and
Luxembourg, and parts of the Netherlands." [[cite:breakout/32/p688]]

On 11 September an American patrol crossed into Germany near the village of Stalzemburg, at 1805 by its report.
[[cite:siegfried/1/p3]]

## What it meant

"The end of the war in Europe seemed just around the corner". To some observers "it seemed that the Allies were
closer to victory after the pursuit in 1944 than after Marshal Ferdinand Foch's grand autumn offensive, which had
preceded German surrender in World War I." [[cite:breakout/32/p702]]
