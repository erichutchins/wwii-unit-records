## What it was for

The landings were to put the Allies ashore in French North Africa "as a step preliminary to seizing the easternmost
protectorate, Tunisia." [[cite:howe-nwa/13/p229]]

"Algiers was the most important objective of Operation TORCH." Its port, railroad terminal, room for a supply base
and two airfields made it a prize; its buildings made it the likeliest home for Allied Force Headquarters; and it
was the seat of government for all French North Africa. [[cite:howe-nwa/13/p229]]
