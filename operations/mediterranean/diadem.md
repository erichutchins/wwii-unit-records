## What happened

At 2300 on 11 May 1944 the guns opened "from the slopes of Monte Cassino to the Tyrrhenian Sea." By 16 May the
French and II Corps were through the Gustav Line; Rome fell on 4 June. [[cite:fisher/p3]] [[cite:fisher/p56]]
