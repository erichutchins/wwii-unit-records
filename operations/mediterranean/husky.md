## What it was for

The invasion of Sicily was decided at Casablanca in January 1943. General Marshall, who had wanted to cross the
Channel that year, "was more concerned with the security of Mediterranean shipping and with the immediate effects of
operations against Germany, however indirect, than he was with eliminating Italy from the war."
[[cite:garland-smyth/1/p1]] [[cite:garland-smyth/1/p9]] [[cite:garland-smyth/1/p10]]

## What it meant

On 25 July, two weeks into the campaign, the King of Italy accepted Mussolini's resignation and appointed Marshal
Badoglio head of government. Badoglio's first proclamation told Italy and the world that "the war continues."
[[cite:garland-smyth/15/p281]]

The campaign ended at Messina on 17 August, after "thirty-eight days of fighting." [[cite:garland-smyth/21/p416]]
