# The 20th Battalion's other stations and smaller jobs, in the order it reached them: places it trained, staged or
# passed through, and work too small or too thinly recorded to be a main project. `station` is a place in
# ../places.csv (the page flies there); none where the place isn't drawn.
unit: 20ncb
stations:
  - place: Norfolk, Va.
    station: Norfolk
    from: 1942-08-20
    to: 1942-09-14
    what: Formed from the Naval Construction Training Center; left by train for Port Hueneme.
    ref: [rg24-190273312/p0041, ncbhist-020/p2]
  - place: Port Hueneme, Calif.
    station: Port Hueneme
    from: 1942-09-28
    to: 1942-11-29
    what: The Advance Base Depot, ready 28 September; the second wave sailed from San Pedro on 29 November.
    ref: [ncbhist-020/p2, ncbhist-020/p7]
  - place: Koli Point, Guadalcanal, and Renard Sound, the Russells
    station: Renard Sound
    from: 1943-06-11
    to: 1943-06-29
    what: The Rear Echelon unloaded at Koli Point and staged in the Russells for the New Georgia landings.
    ref: [rg38-78205374/p0012, ncbhist-020/p11]
  - place: Oloana Bay, Vangunu (Wickham Anchorage)
    station: Oloana Bay
    from: 1943-06-30
    to: 1943-10-15
    what: >-
      Landed with the Vangunu force, about 400 men; what the group built there isn't in the records read. Wickham
      had its own naval base commander by September 1943: on 8 September the patrol craft APc 37, 42 and 46 ran
      from Wickham Anchorage to Segi, and LCTs 145 and 146 loaded half a company of the 20th at "Oliana" for Munda.
      Commander Naval Base Wickham's own diary is not in the catalog's war diaries, and the 1944 Forward Area and
      New Georgia base diaries don't name Wickham: its later use isn't known.
    ref: [ncbhist-020/p2, ncbhist-020/p12, https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/USMC/USMC-M-CSol/USMC-M-CSol-2.html, rg38-134319760/p0034]
  - place: Roviana Lagoon and Munda, New Georgia
    station: Roviana
    from: 1943-09-09
    to: 1943-11-05
    what: The Viru company's echelons, at East Rovianna and also working at Munda, before joining the Bau unit.
    ref: [ncbhist-020/p12]
  - place: Doke Doke Island boat pool, New Georgia
    from: 1943-10-01
    to: 1943-12-31
    what: >-
      Camp and administration buildings, roads, three coral-log wharves, a fuel-storage area and fuelling pier, and
      quonset-hut warehouses, in October 1943; 1 officer and 15 men still there at the end of December.
    ref: [bases-25, ncbhist-020/p3]
  - place: Ondonga, New Georgia
    station: Ondonga
    from: 1943-10-15
    to: 1943-12-31
    what: Three LCT landings and a 40-foot boat-pool landing, October-November 1943; the section's headquarters by December.
    ref: [bases-25, ncbhist-020/p12, ncbhist-020/p3]
  - place: Milne Bay and Brisbane
    station: Brisbane
    from: 1944-04-17
    to: 1944-06-02
    what: The Forward Echelon on its way from Woodlark to the Russells, quartered at Camp Seabee near Brisbane.
    ref: [ncbhist-020/p3]
  - place: Camp Parks, Calif., and Davisville, R.I.
    station: Camp Parks
    from: 1944-09-29
    to: 1945-02-01
    what: Home from the Russells; leave from Davisville, then re-formed at Camp Parks.
    ref: [ncbhist-020/p4, rg24-190273312/p0553, rg24-190273312/p0581]
  - place: Saipan
    station: Saipan
    from: 1945-03-04
    to: 1945-05-07
    what: Staging for Okinawa; the battalion put up a building for an Army Coca-Cola plant.
    ref: [ncbhist-020/p4, ncbhist-020/p9, ncbhist-020/p12]
