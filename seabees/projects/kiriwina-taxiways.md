## Built

"In September 1943, at the request of the 6th Army, 12 officers and 306 men of the 20th Battalion were sent to
Kiriwina to assist in the airfield development" (the day of the month is assumed). Their principal task was two
taxiways, "one 7,000 feet long, with 25 fighter hardstands; the other, 5,300 feet long, with 16 bomber hardstands",
then operations buildings and camps; they were done by 28 October 1943 and went back to Woodlark. [[cite:bases-26]]
[[cite:ncbhist-020/p2]]

## Afterwards

*Secondary.* "The first taxiway was completed on October 12, two days ahead of schedule, in time to support a major
air raid against Rabaul." Kiriwina was meant as "an air base to support future moves against the Japanese in New
Guinea". The raid and the squadrons are context; no record read ties a unit to the 20th's taxiways. [[cite:bases-26]]

*Not known.* Which squadrons used the hardstands, from a record of their own.
