## Built

The Second Section reached Banika on 10 January 1944 "to operate the ABCD annex": receiving and storing material,
assembling and shipping it, "uncrating and assembling of jeeps, command cars, water trailers, and fire pumps;
erecting, checking, and servicing of cranes, shovels, and bulldozers". The First Section joined it in June 1944, and
the battalion sailed for home on 14 September. [[cite:bases-25]] [[cite:ncbhist-020/p12]] [[cite:ncbhist-020/p3]]

The Russells base's diary for 16-23 January 1944: "Work on foundations for eight warehouses at ABCD ANNEX and
foundations for twelve warehouses for the FOURTH MARINE BASE DEPOT continued." [[cite:rg38-78299723/p0001]]

## Afterwards

*Secondary.* The 24th Battalion enlarged the annex in June 1944, and CBMU 501 took over its operation in January
1945. [[cite:bases-25]]

*Secondary.* The 22nd Construction Regiment, staging in the Russells, was told to draw what it needed "from stocks
available in the Russells or on Guadalcanal" before landing on Green Island on 15 February 1944. The annex isn't
named: that the regiment drew on it is a lead, not a finding. [[cite:bases-25]]

*Not known.* Which units or landings were equipped through the annex. The catalog's Russells diaries skip February
1944 to May 1945.
