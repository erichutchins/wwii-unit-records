## Built

The Oloana Bay group's first echelon reached Bau on 10 September 1943 and the Viru company joined it on 5 November;
"Unit at Bau consists of 4 officers and 195 men" on 31 December, and all of them sailed for the Russells on 9
January 1944. [[cite:ncbhist-020/p11]] [[cite:ncbhist-020/p12]] [[cite:ncbhist-020/p3]]

"On Bau Island, beginning in October 1943, a PT-boat base was built by the 20th Battalion. The Seabees first
improved docking facilities, built roads and incidental structures. In November, facilities were increased to
include three steel arch-rib warehouses, four quonset huts, and water and electrical systems." [[cite:bases-25]]

The PT command's history: when its commander "decided on about 1 October to move Motor Torpedo Boat Base Eleven in
and establish his headquarters there, a small Construction Battalion Unit worked a month in clearing, excavating and
filling in a site for base construction". It does not name the battalion. [[cite:rg38-77668666/p0081]]

## Afterwards

*Record.* "PT Base 11 was established prior to January 1944 on Bau Island adjacent to Rendova, B.S.I. It was a major
overhaul and repair base and was considered the headquarters base." [[cite:rg38-77668666/p0151]]

*Record.* Motor Torpedo Boat Squadron 19's nine boats were "moored at PT Base Eleven, Rendova, BSI" on 1 March 1944,
before leaving for Green Island. [[cite:rg38-78404088/p0002]]

*Record.* The PT history describes the base: "Large pontoon docks, flanked by a torpedo dock and three drydocks, were
installed directly in front of ComMTBRons headquarters"; as many as ten boats tied up at the docks by day, and at night
they were dispersed to buoys "between Bau and Rendova Islands". From 1 January to 15 June 1944 Bau was also "ideally
suited as a pre-combat training center": torpedo-firing, anti-barge, destroyer-PT and PT-Catalina exercises in Blanche
Channel, with a measured mile marked "just before the channel entrance to Bau Island". [[cite:rg38-77668666/p0082]]
[[cite:rg38-77668666/p0123]]

*Not known.* The day the PT command moved to Bau. Squadron 5's diaries, December 1943 to April 1944, searched by OCR,
say "PT Base Rendova" and never Bau or Lumbari. [[cite:rg38-78332782/p0003]]

*Record.* The end, from the New Georgia naval base's diary: at the end of May 1944, "Plans were underway to remove
Naval Base personnel from Rendova Harbor to Munda, leaving only a small pilot station on Bau Island". In 8–15 June,
"Movement of ComMTBronsPac to Torokina together with the departure of all squadrons previously based at Rendova
indicated an early removal of MTB Base Eleven to a more forward area"; in 16–23 June "MTB Base Eleven at Rendova
received orders alerting that activity for early movement". [[cite:rg38-78477045/p0012]] [[cite:rg38-78511824/p0002]]

*Secondary.* In January 1944 the Rendova PT base "was moved from Lumbari Island to Bau Island", became "the main
repair and overhaul base in the area", and was Commodore Moran's headquarters until 15 June 1944; the patrols
themselves ran from forward bases. [[cite:https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/USN/CloseQuarters/PT-3.html]]

*Inference.* The "small Construction Battalion Unit" of the PT history was the 20th's Bau unit: its own dates on Bau
match, and *Building the Navy's Bases* names the 20th for the base. The boats came after it left; their patrols are
context, not the battalion's act. [[cite:ncbhist-020/p11]] [[cite:bases-25]]
