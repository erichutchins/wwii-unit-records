## Built

The first echelon came ashore on Gushikawa Beach, Chimu Wan, on 18 April 1945; by 20 April the 36th Battalion was
improving the "road between our camp and that of 20th NCB". [[cite:rg367-83572565/p0002]] [[cite:ncbhist-036/p9]]

The battalion's narrative, written afterwards: "On Okinawa the 20th NCB constructed and maintained roads, built a
bridge, constructed waterfront facilities, maintained pontoon barges and worked on the Awashi Airfield, as well as an
advanced base construction Depot located on Ketchin Hento." It gives no dates. [[cite:ncbhist-020/p9]]

The battalion was broken up on 29 August 1945, its men transferred to the 15th, 36th, 79th and 126th Battalions.
[[cite:rg24-190273312/p0835]]

## Afterwards

*Secondary.* "Pontoon structures were used more extensively at Okinawa than in any other previous Pacific
operation"; pontoon piers at Katchin Hanto, Awase and Kuba Saki were in operation in May 1945. Which of them the 20th
worked on is not said. [[cite:bases-30]]

*Secondary.* Katchin Hanto became a seaplane base, a section base, a fuel pier and a general cargo pier; an advance
base construction depot "included a spare parts depot and an automotive construction equipment overhaul and repair
depot". The Katchin Hanto section base was still being developed after the surrender. This was Okinawa's build-up
for the invasion of Japan, the work of many battalions: context for the 20th's depot, not its own account.
[[cite:bases-30]]

*Not known.* Which pier, road or bridge was the 20th's, and what used them.
