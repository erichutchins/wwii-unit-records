## Built

The first wave, about 600 men, reached Nouméa on 20 or 21 October 1942 and "concentrated their attention on
stevedoring activities"; unloading in the first half of December averaged 5,000 tons a day, "still not sufficient to
keep up with the rate at which cargo was arriving". [[cite:bases-24]] [[cite:ncbhist-020/p2]]

On 20 January 1943 the battalion's card has it "engaged in stevedore work and upon relief by 2nd CB Special will
engage construction projects in White Poppy area"; what it built there is not described. Half the battalion left on
13 May 1943; the rest sailed for Guadalcanal on 7 June. [[cite:ncbhist-020/p2]] [[cite:rg38-78205374/p0005]]

## Afterwards

*Secondary.* Nouméa "was developed as the main fleet base in the South Pacific", "served as a staging area for the
development of other advance bases, such as Guadalcanal", and from 8 November 1942 was the headquarters of the Allied
commander of the South Pacific. That is the port the battalion unloaded for: context, not the battalion's own work.
[[cite:bases-24]]

*Not known.* Which ships, cargoes or units the battalion's gangs handled is not in the records read.
