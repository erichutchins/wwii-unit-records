## Built

One company of the 20th went in with the Viru Harbor landing to build a base for PT boats; driven off on 30 June,
it landed on 2 July 1943. [[cite:bases-25]] [[cite:ncbhist-020/p2]] [[cite:https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/USMC/USMC-M-CSol/USMC-M-CSol-2.html]]

"The original plan for the establishment of a PT-boat base was abandoned because the harbor was found to be
unsuitable." The Seabees stayed on minor construction and maintenance: "A marine railway was constructed for the
repair of small landing craft, and an existing wharf, 50 feet by 30 feet, was rehabilitated." [[cite:bases-25]]

The company left Viru in echelons on 5, 6 and 9 September 1943 by the battalion's chronology; *Building the Navy's
Bases* says October. [[cite:ncbhist-020/p12]] [[cite:bases-25]]

## Afterwards

*Secondary.* There was no PT base at Viru: the plan was dropped. [[cite:bases-25]]

*Not known.* Which landing craft or flotillas used the railway and the wharf. A search of the catalog's war diaries found no
South Pacific LCT flotilla diary for 1943-44 and no Viru base diary, and
Viru isn't named in the Forward Area's diaries for February and April 1944 or the New Georgia base's for May and June
1944 (searched by OCR, 1 Nov 2026).
