## Built

About 500 men of the 20th landed with the Woodlark task force on 30 June 1943, attached to the naval base unit, and
"were assigned the construction of housing, roads, and services"; the 60th Battalion built the airfield. By October
"a 30-mile network of good hard roads had been completed". The Seabees also put up eight prefabricated buildings and
nine quonset huts for the Army Air Force and a small hospital, assembled pontoon barges and a PT-boat pier, and set
up a sawmill. [[cite:bases-26]] [[cite:ncbhist-020/p2]]

Construction stopped on 7 January 1944 to prepare to leave, and the echelon sailed for Milne Bay on 16 April 1944.
*Building the Navy's Bases* has all Seabees but the 60th's maintenance men detached from Woodlark by 1 November
1943; the battalion's own card keeps the echelon there into April 1944. [[cite:ncbhist-020/p3]] [[cite:bases-26]]
