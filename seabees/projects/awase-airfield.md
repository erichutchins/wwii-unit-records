## Built

"On 29 June, the batt moved from its bivouac area near Gusikawa into a permanent area about a mile from the Awashi
Airstrip." The battalion's narrative says it "worked on the Awashi Airfield", without a date; its monthly cards
record no airfield work. [[cite:ncbhist-020/p5]] [[cite:ncbhist-020/p9]]

*Building the Navy's Bases* gives the strip to the 36th Battalion, from 23 April; after the May rains "Six battalions
cooperated in moving coral to the field from the quarries at Gushikawa and Myazato", unnamed. The 20th was camped
near Gushikawa then; that it was one of the six is an inference, not a record. [[cite:bases-30]]

Commander Naval Air Bases Okinawa's diary names no battalion for the runway: on 26 June "CBMU-624 at AWASE was
constructing the bivouac area", and on 30 June "At AWASE rapid progress was made toward completion of the runway".
[[cite:rg38-77471094/p0029]] [[cite:rg38-77471094/p0033]]

## Afterwards

*Record.* "Effective 1200 this date Marine Air Base AWASE is activated" (30 June 1945). [[cite:rg38-77471094/p0033]]

*Record.* On 1 July "MAG-14 ... moved to AWASE Base for future operations"; "The first combat plane to land at AWASE
landed at 1329, and was followed by ninety five (95) additional aircraft of MAG-14". On 15 July "MAG-33 moved this
date from KADENA to AWASE". [[cite:rg38-77494684/p0002]] [[cite:rg38-77494684/p0016]]

*Record.* MAG-14's July diary, at "Awase Airfield": Marine Fighting Squadrons 212, 222 and 223, with the torpedo
squadrons VMTB-232 and VMTB-131 attached, flying combat air patrols over Okinawa and strikes on Sakishima Gunto and
southern Kyushu. [[cite:rg38-77487065/p0003]] [[cite:rg38-77487065/p0004]]

*Secondary.* *Building the Navy's Bases* has "The first planes of Marine Air Group 33" land on 30 June; the air
bases' diary has MAG-14 first, on 1 July, and MAG-33 from 15 July. The diary is the record. [[cite:bases-30]]

*Inference.* These squadrons' missions are context: a squadron flying from a strip isn't the battalion's act, and no
record ties a mission to the 20th's part of the work. [[cite:ncbhist-020/p9]] [[cite:rg38-77494684/p0002]]
